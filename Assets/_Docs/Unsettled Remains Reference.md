## Промпт

Make a SINGLE FRONT full-body modeling view in a clear anatomical A-POSE, on a plain uniform LIGHT warm-gray background like the other project's character references. Preserve the same desiccated undead North Caucasus warrior identity and ALL existing design details: skeletal dried face, aged pointed iron helmet with nasal guard, ragged chainmail aventail and shoulder mail, dark decayed tunic with chest cartridge holders, crossed worn leather straps, decorated waist belt without any weapon, shield or scabbard, engraved metal bracers, dried bony hands, long shredded dark-brown and muted burgundy fabric skirt strips, trousers, shin wraps and worn boots. Do not add or remove equipment, do not turn into a clean living human, do not add magical effects. Pose: upright neutral torso and level head looking directly forward, strictly frontal orthographic camera, bilaterally matching limb proportions. Arms straight diagonally downward 40 degrees from vertical torso, almost straight elbows, neutral wrists, relaxed hands with five distinct gently separated fingers, large clear gaps between hands and hips; NOT arms hanging near the body, NOT bent elbows, NOT T-pose. Legs straight hip-width apart, feet pointing forward. Entire helmet tip, fingertips and boots visible with generous margin. Soft EVEN studio light revealing dark garments and anatomy clearly without changing their dark material colors; no dramatic shadows or rim light. Same stylized hand-crafted dark-fantasy painterly reference rendering, matte aged natural materials. ONE figure only, no side/back views, no inserts, no text, no labels, no frames, no scenery, no pedestal. Landscape or square canvas as needed so outstretched arms are not cropped. Maximum dimension 2048 pixels.
